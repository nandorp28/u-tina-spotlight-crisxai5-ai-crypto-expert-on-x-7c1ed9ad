A make.fun bounty entry: an original spotlight graphic introducing
[@crisxai5](https://x.com/crisxai5) as a must-follow AI & crypto expert on X,
and the X post that carries it. Places go by the post's likes + reposts +
quotes at the deadline; the top 10 share $2 USDC.

## The graphic

`spotlight.png` (1200×675, drawn from scratch with Pillow by `graphic.py`):
a dark-navy spotlight card — hero handle `@crisxai5`, "AI × CRYPTO EXPERT",
the tagline "The follow that filters the noise.", four topic cards (AI agents,
crypto insights, Web3 innovation, on-chain automation), an "ON THE FEED"
panel, a candlestick + rising-sparkline strip, a ticker tape, and a
FOLLOW @crisxai5 call to action. No fabricated stats: everything on the card
comes from the bounty's own brief.

## The post draft

Posted from [@jonastama7](https://x.com/jonastama7) with the graphic attached
(229/280 counted chars — URLs count 23 on X):

> Stop scrolling CT for noise.
>
> @crisxai5 is the follow that filters it — AI agents, crypto alpha & Web3, explained like a human, not a hype bot.
>
> https://x.com/crisxai5
>
> #AI #Crypto #AIAgents #Web3
> makefun-sunset-sandbar-knapsack

`POST.json` holds the post's address once it is posted; `main.ts` copies it
to `/output/POST.json`, which is all the program has to do.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Regenerate the graphic with `python3 graphic.py` (needs Pillow).
